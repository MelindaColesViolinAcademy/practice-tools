How to use it

  • Find a quiet room. Background noise confuses any tuner.
  • Bow slowly and steadily, near the middle of the bow.
  • Tune one string at a time and let the note ring.
  • When it says in tune, stop and listen — that is the sound you are learning.
  • Tune down to the note, then up to it. Coming up to pitch holds better.
Tune slowly. Listen carefully. Build beautiful habits from the beginning.

A word of caution

If a string feels very tight or the peg will not hold, stop and ask rather than forcing it. Strings do break, and a snapping E string is startling. Fine tuners at the tailpiece are for small adjustments; the pegs are for larger ones.

This tuner needs microphone access because it listens to your violin. Nothing is recorded, stored or sent anywhere — the sound is analysed on your own device and then discarded.
