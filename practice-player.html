How to use it well

  • Loop just the bar that goes wrong, not the whole tune. Two bars at most.
  • Slow it until you can play it correctly, however slow that turns out to be.
  • Three correct times in a row, then raise the speed a little.
  • If it falls apart, drop back down. Going backwards is part of it.
  • Ten minutes of this beats an hour of playing a piece through from the top.
Practising a passage fast and wrong just teaches you to play it wrong quickly.
