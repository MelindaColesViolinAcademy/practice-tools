First position notes

The pattern every beginner learns first. Fourth finger gives the same note as the next open string — which is how you check you are in tune. Second finger is high throughout.

G stringG · A · B · C · D
D stringD · E · F♯ · G · A
A stringA · B · C♯ · D · E
E stringE · F♯ · G♯ · A · B

B, F♯, C♯ and G♯ are all high second fingers. In this pattern the second finger sits high — right up beside the third finger, close enough to touch. The gap between first and second is wide; the gap between second and third is tiny. Getting that shape into your hand is most of what first position is.

There is also a low second finger, giving F natural on the D string or C natural on the A. You will meet it soon enough. Everything on this page is high two.

Play fourth finger on the D string, then the open A. They should sound the same. If they do not, your hand has drifted.
