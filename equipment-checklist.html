Before you begin

A tuned instrument, the right few accessories and a comfortable setup make learning at home far easier. Have everything within reach before you start, so you are not stopping halfway through to look for something.

Have these ready first

  • Violin, bow, case, rosin and shoulder rest
  • Music stand and cleaning cloth
  • Tuner or metronome
  • Method book and a pencil

Useful once you are going

  • Spare set of strings
  • Practice mute
  • Fingerboard tape
  • Case humidifier
Keep your violin, bow, rosin and music stand together in one corner of a room. The less setting up you have to do, the more you will practise.
