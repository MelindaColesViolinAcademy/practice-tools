Building the bow hold

Four steps, in order. Build it, take it apart, build it again.

  1. Thumb bends softly
  2. Fingers curve, not stiff
  3. Little finger curved on top
  4. Hand stays flexible

Five short tasks. The whole thing takes about ten minutes.
