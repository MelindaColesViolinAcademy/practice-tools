What the chart is really for

Not discipline, and not guilt. It is there because progress on the violin is invisible day to day and obvious over a month — and on the days it feels like nothing is happening, a chart with fourteen squares filled in is the evidence that it is.

Three days a week, every week, will take you further than seven days once a month.
