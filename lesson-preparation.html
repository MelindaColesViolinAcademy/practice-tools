What we are doing today

A good session usually comes back to the same four things — posture, sound, timing and consistency.

  • Set the violin and bow up with less tension
  • Revisit open-string tone and legato bowing
  • Steady the rhythm, counting the subdivisions
  • Bring the left hand back together with the bow
  • Finish with one clear thing to work on this week
